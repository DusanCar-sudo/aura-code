Seasons & Monsoon

When Does the Monsoon Start?

Da Nang's weather is governed by two monsoons. The northeast monsoon brings the rainy season from September to December, while the southwest summer monsoon brings surprisingly little rain to this part of the central coast.

JanFebMarAprMayJunJulAug ← Monsoon StartSepOctNovDec

Dry Season

February – April
  • Least rainfall (20–35 mm/month)
  • Pleasant temperatures 23–27°C
  • 6–7 hours of sunshine daily
  • Best time for outdoor activities

Monsoon / Rainy Season

August – December
  • Brings 70% of annual rainfall
  • October peak: 650 mm, 20 rainy days
  • Northeast monsoon drives heavy rain
  • Typhoon risk Sep–Nov
Sep – Dec

Northeast Monsoon

The primary rainy season. Cool, moist air from the northeast hits the Annamite Range, releasing torrential rain. October is the wettest month with 650 mm of rainfall across 20 days — nearly a third of the year's total.

May – Aug

Southwest Monsoon

Surprisingly dry for Da Nang. The Truong Son mountain range blocks moisture from the southwest, creating a rain shadow. This period is hot (34–35°C) but relatively sunny with only 85–115 mm/month.

Aug → Dec

Typhoon Season

Da Nang sits in the path of Pacific typhoons from late August to mid-December. The coast is the highest-risk zone. Typhoons can bring extreme winds, storm surges, and 300+ mm of rain in a single day.

Best Time to Visit

Late January to mid-March — the last rains fade, temperatures are comfortable (22–26°C), humidity drops, and the sea is calm. February to April offers the most reliable sunny weather.
